## Scope

Re-run the existing Lara (3 traits) and King (4) H-MAGMA analyses
using the 13,376 revised putative regulatory loops selected with a 2,001-bp
TSS window, full loop-anchor intervals, and minimum 50-bp ATAC support.
Kuhn, Gunturkun, and additional cohorts are excluded from the main seven-trait analysis.

one-trait run (N=1,317), not added to the seven-trait BH testing family.

statistics; the original upstream accession was not independently established

**Provenance correction:** Lara and King use deposited GWAS summary
statistics (King lifted from rn6 to rn7). The existing Kuhn inputs were
generated by our earlier local GWAS reproduction, not downloaded
author-provided SNP-level summary statistics. Checks of selected paper/
report loci agreed closely, but this is not proof of whole-genome identity.
At the user's request, only Lara/King are retained. Task-generated Kuhn
outputs are archived outside the final output folder under
revision_2001bp_excluded_Kuhn; they are not included in tables or FDR correction.

Only the input loop set is replaced. The same pre-existing GWAS files, sample sizes,
rn7 LD reference individuals/genotypes, MAGMA v1.08 SNPwise-mean model,
protein-coding gene definitions, direct exon/promoter SNP mapping, and
existing distal-ATAC SNP filters are retained. No cMAGMA rerun.

**The questions are whether the revised loops add previously unassigned
non-coding SNPs or new SNP-gene connections, and whether updated gene-level
association results differ from those obtained using legacy loops.**

## Definitions

- 2,001 bp (TSS +/-1,000 bp) defines the revised input loop set.
- The unchanged H-MAGMA annotation step uses its original transcript
  promoters: strand-aware upstream 2,000 / downstream 500 bp, inclusive.
  It considers both loop orientations, as the original code did.
  Its gene assignments therefore need not equal the revision's direct
  2,001-bp loop-gene list.
- The original SNP filter removes every direct exon/promoter SNP from
  Hi-C reassignment and restricts the remaining SNPs to distal
  Duttke/Telese ATAC signal after the original TSS/promoter exclusions.
- The 50-bp criterion is a loop-selection criterion, not a new requirement
  for a one-base SNP to overlap an ATAC peak by 50 bp.
- A previously unassigned SNP was absent from the entire old annotation.
  A new SNP-gene pair may involve such a SNP or an already annotated SNP
  assigned to an additional gene.

## Verification

1. Preserve the original exon/promoter baseline exactly.
2. Recalculate the legacy Hi-C branch first: all 323,827 original
   SNP-gene pairs must match, with zero extra/missing pairs.
3. Verify 13,376 revised loops and inclusive 5/10/25-kb anchor widths.
4. Use normalized loop coordinates directly; do not add another base.
5. Read GWAS paths, N, and LD reference from original MAGMA logs.
6. Check unique SNP IDs and valid P-values without modifying GWAS files.
7. Confirm successful MAGMA completion and unique gene IDs.
8. Compare unchanged SNP annotations with their old gene statistics.
9. Recompute BH and Bonferroni correction separately within each trait,
   using all successfully tested genes, not just loop-associated genes.
   Provide exploratory BH correction across the seven retained traits.

## Limitations

H-MAGMA gives gene-level association evidence, not direct statistical tests
of individual loops, causal variants, enhancer function, or loop-set
enrichment. SNP GWAS P-values do not change. Smaller gene P-values or more
significant genes do not by themselves prove better annotation. Correlated
genes can share GWAS loci and SNPs.
The previously unassigned SNP counts refer to SNPs with a GWAS P-value
and a reference-level loop assignment, not a guarantee that every SNP
survives all MAGMA internal QC. NSNPS reports the final gene-level SNP count.
"Non-coding" here means outside the retained protein-coding exon/promoter
annotation, not a new functional classification of every variant.

The pre-existing GWAS, liftOver, HS LD reference, pooled cortex Hi-C, and
non-matched PFC snATAC limitations remain. The existing King inputs retain
their original handling of two many-to-one liftOver targets; this rerun
does not silently alter that preprocessing. The seven traits reproduce the
previous selected Lara/King scope, not every phenotype in each publication.

Method reference: [H-MAGMA protocol](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10026181/).
